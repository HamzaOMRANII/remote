NEXT FEATURE — M7 CAMERA DISCOVERY ON MY NETWORK (decided 2026-11-11 with the owner, in session 2efdda84)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Owner's idea: "when I am connected to my access point I should discover all my cameras in the network and
access them from the open ports". Owner's answers: build it in BOTH the Android app and the laptop/Pi agent;
watch at home AND from anywhere (relay to the dashboard); build it in THIS session (remote_app_session).

Design agreed:
 1. "My network": the owner marks his Wi-Fi once (SSID/BSSID saved); discovery only runs on a trusted network,
    never on public Wi-Fi. (Android: needs location permission to read the SSID.)
 2. Discovery (owned devices only):
    - ONVIF WS-Discovery: UDP multicast 239.255.255.250:3702 Probe -> ProbeMatch (XAddrs, model, brand).
    - Port check on the local /24 (from the phone's IP + netmask): 554, 8554 (RTSP), 80, 8080, 443 (web UI),
      8000 (Hikvision), 37777 (Dahua), 1935 (RTMP). Short timeouts, limited parallelism.
    - Result list: IP, MAC vendor if known, brand/model (ONVIF), open ports, guessed type.
 3. Access: the owner types the camera's OWN username/password once (stored encrypted on the phone /
    agent config). NO default-password guessing or brute force (security + legal; tell the owner why).
    - RTSP (554/8554): play the stream; ONVIF GetStreamUri to find the right path, else common paths.
    - Web port (80/8080/443): open the camera's own page in an in-app view.
 4. From anywhere: "Add to dashboard" provisions the camera as its own device (like remote_agent cameras) and
    the phone (spare phone at home) or laptop/Pi relays RTSP -> WebRTC through the existing TURN.
 Order: Android app first (discovery + local view + relay), then the same discovery in remote_agent
 (extend agent/scan.py: ONVIF + more ports; relay already exists in agent/camera.py).
 Testing: the owner has real cameras at home; Claude has none -> test with a synthetic RTSP source
 (e.g. ffmpeg/mediamtx on the box) and ask the owner to test on his Wi-Fi.
